About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Utah, advised by Professor Haitao Wang. I expect to graduate in May 2027.

What draws me to computational geometry is the moment when a geometric observation reveals a new way to solve a difficult problem. I enjoy both developing those ideas in my research and explaining them in the classroom.

Before joining the University of Utah, I pursued doctoral coursework at Utah State University and earned my M.S. and B.S. in Applied Mathematics and Informatics from Novosibirsk State University.

Research Interests

My research explores how geometric structure can lead to efficient algorithms for classical problems in graph theory and geometric optimization. My current work studies dominating set, independent set, clique, discrete k-center, and dispersion problems in settings involving points or disks in convex position.
